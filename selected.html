Selected Works

Selected Haiku

Twenty poems from the complete archive.

The complete archive contains 203 haiku written between 2001 and 2026. This selection offers a guided path through the work: the war remembered, the boat journey, the monastery, the road home, and the years of quiet attention at the end.

The poems are presented in chronological order, numbered as in the full archive. Read the paired poems in sequence (such as the crow on a bare branch followed by the crow in falling snow) to witness how the teaching learned to hide inside the image.
